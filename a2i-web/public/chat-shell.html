1 OpenCode Zen (free — no card) Recommended

Frontier free models (Big Pickle, DeepSeek V4 Flash Free, … — see MODELS.md). Get a key free with opencode auth login --provider zen or on opencode.ai, paste it here and press Test key. Free tier: 100 requests/day.

2 Gemini (Google)

Paste your own key — free from aistudio.google.com/apikey. Stored only in this browser, sent directly to Google.

3 Local model file — SSD / disk (no download)

Keep a GGUF model on your SSD and load it straight from disk — no download, works offline, and new preview URLs never re-download. Big models fit too. Get GGUF files from Hugging Face (Qwen, Llama, …).

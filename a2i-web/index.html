AI providers — local & free

Famous, strong models for free: get a key, paste it, Add. Keys stay in this browser.
• Groq — console.groq.com/keys (Llama 3.3 70B, very fast)
• OpenRouter — openrouter.ai/keys (DeepSeek R1, Llama 70B — :free)
